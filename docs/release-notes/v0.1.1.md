A fix release. **Download `GrammarBuddy-0.1.1.dmg` below** and drag it over the old copy in Applications. Signed and notarized; macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon.

## Fixed

- **Word: "Insert below" always failed.** After translating in Word, the button ended in "Couldn't insert; you can copy the result". The translation is now inserted as a new paragraph right after the original. If Word ever refuses, the panel shows Word's own message.

## Changed

- **A simpler panel.** The footer has the one main action and Copy. The view switch ("Changes | Result", "Side by side | Translation") and the extra "Below" button are gone.
- **Polish, Tone and Translate show only the new text.** Fix still marks every change in the text so each one can be switched off. The original stays one click away under "Original".

## 修复

- **Word 里「插到下方」总是失败。** 在 Word 里翻译后点这个按钮，会提示「没能插入，可以复制结果」。现在译文会作为新段落插在原文后面。如果 Word 拒绝，面板会显示 Word 给的原因。

## 变化

- **面板更简单。** 底部只有一个主按钮和「复制」。视图切换（「差异 | 结果」「对照 | 译文」）和多出来的「插到下方」按钮都去掉了。
- **润色、语气、翻译只显示新文字。** 改错仍然在文字里逐处标出修改，可以逐条取消。原文在上方「Original」一行里，点开可看。
